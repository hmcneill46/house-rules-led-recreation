## Cheaper strain relief, with the same assembly method

CPC reduces the shared adhesive/tie basket from the earlier **£18.48 Farnell allowance to £9.83 delivered**, saving **£8.65 for every batch size**. The calculation is £3.92 adhesive + £0.52 ties + £3.75 online small-order handling = £8.19 excluding VAT, or £9.828 rounded to £9.83 including VAT. Separate unit-VAT rounding could make the final bill 1p lower. The online handling charge is stated in [CPC's purchase terms](https://cpc.farnell.com/terms-of-purchase); no express delivery is selected.

The clear EGS10C-20G is an electronics-grade, non-corrosive, non-conductive, flexible silicone intended for corrosion-sensitive electronics. It replaces the hidden white EGS bead in procurement; the [manufacturer lists both in the same EGS range](https://www.chipquik.com/store/index.php?cPath=490_492). After electrical checks, use a small bead to retain the insulated loom to main-board soldermask, away from pads and components, as the current assembly guide requires. The printed rear-cover anchor and cable tie remain necessary. Allow the bead to cure before moving the loom; keep it small enough to cut or peel away carefully for servicing.

This does not change CAD or remove the main-board pad protection. Tape by itself was not substituted: its resistance to peeling by this loom has not been physically qualified. Ordinary acetic-cure bathroom silicone was not substituted either. The cheaper black/white CPC EGS tubes were on backorder, so the in-stock clear tube is used. The new 2.5 mm ties fit the existing 2.7 mm passage; Pi Hut's in-stock 180 mm ties are 4.6 mm wide and unsuitable.

## Fit and assembly requirements retained

The selected wire is 28 AWG stranded (7/0.120 mm), maximum outside diameter **0.71 mm**. It retains the preferred power-wire gauge and fits the existing space reservation. Start with about 14 × 70 mm MCU wires and 6 × 45 mm USB wires per display, then trim after a dry fit. The bought lengths leave a modest spare allowance. Keep D+/D− short, adjacent and twisted as the existing assembly guide specifies. Typical cheap 28 AWG silicone wire with 1.2 mm outside diameter was rejected.

Screw heads must stay within **4.0 mm diameter / 1.5 mm height**. The specified M2 button screws need the 1.3 mm key. Generic M2 DIN 7985 pan heads can be 1.6 mm high and were not substituted. Screw quantity promotions were investigated, but the actual carts returned zero discount, so none is deducted. CPC's 50-piece button-screw packs were also checked and cost more than the required small quantities from Bolt Base.

Factory assembly covers the small parts on the custom main PCB. The owner still fits the seven LEDs, solders the 20-wire loom and assembles the case: approximately **84 LED joints + 40 wire joints per display**, followed by inspection, electrical checks and firmware loading. Purchased stock modules and the actual USB plug need the receipt checks in the assembly guide. Alignment jigs and 3 mm spacers can use the already-owned filament. No purchased spacer kit or extra filament spool is charged.
