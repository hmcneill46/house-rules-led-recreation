# XIAO RP2040 Plus USB suspend audit

29 October 2026. Source review of the manufacturer **Plus V1.0** native board/schematic and the B2 firmware against Arduino-Pico **6.3.1**. No hardware current or USB-resume measurement has been made. The 2.5 mA whole-device suspend target remains a **prototype acceptance test**, not a result demonstrated by compilation, low CPU frequency, or this audit.

## Hardware findings

The Plus has no unavoidable multi-milliamp green power LED: **D3 is controlled by GP25 through R21 and Q1**. GP25 drives an N-channel MOSFET gate, so **LOW turns it off; HIGH turns it on**. This is different from old standard-XIAO active-low LED assumptions. The earlier HIGH=off implementation was corrected before the final B2 builds. The updated `initVariant()` drives GP25 low before USB starts. R22 pulls the gate down when undriven. D3's 1.5 kΩ R15 is fed from 3.3 V.

The independent red **D2 charge indicator** has R14=10 kΩ from 3.3 V and the charger's nCHG sink. It is not GPIO-controlled. Its absolute resistor-only bound is 3.3/10k=0.33 mA before LED forward drop; its actual average depends on charger state. It must be included in measured suspend current. The charger's published indicator states are intermittent during charge, then continuous briefly at completion before going high impedance; there is no guaranteed no-battery module current given here. [Seeed Plus schematic](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/XIAO-RP2040/res/XIAO_RP2040-Plus_SCH.pdf), [SG Micro SGM40567 datasheet, pages3–5](https://www.sg-micro.com/rect/assets/e95e555a-a8a2-4761-b86a-6554dff2d8ed/SGM40567.pdf).

GP11 is the RGB supply enable through U10 (TPS22916); GP12 is RGB data. Holding both low removes the WS2812 supply and prevents data back-power. U18's VCCA/OE are also on RGB_EN. GP24/BAT_EN stays low, assisted by R20=100 kΩ, and B2 uses no battery. D3/GP29 is unused. The MCU's onboard RS3236 3.3 V regulator remains enabled by hardware. Its published family quiescent current is tens of microamps, not milliamps. The TPS2116 input mux is 1.32 µA typical. These values describe individual parts, not the complete board's suspend current. [RUNIC RS3236 manufacturer-authored datasheet](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/lcsc/2202251530_Jiangsu-RUNIC-Tech-RS3236-ADJ8YF5_C379351.pdf), [TI TPS2116 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2116.pdf).

SGM40567 tables specify 72 µA typical/110 µA maximum at output fold-back with zero battery current, and 100/135 µA at one stable-charging condition. The often-quoted 15 µA is specifically its diode-charging condition with a 10 kΩ input resistor; it cannot be substituted for an unmeasured, no-battery XIAO budget. The flash, RP2040 silicon/USB PLL, pull-up paths, charger state, external CC sensing and custom-board switch leakage still need to be accounted for at the USB cable.

The native power pins and capacitor nets were independently extracted to `xiao-plus-power-source-audit.json`, including source SHA256. Direct VBUS capacitance on the Plus is C2=1 µF plus C26=0.1 µF. The TPS2116 output VSYS additionally has C5=10 µF, C15=4.7 µF and C14=0.1 µF, followed by 10 µF on 3.3 V and local bypasses. The mux has controlled slew. **Direct capacitance alone does not prove USB inrush compliance**; downstream charging, the custom-board load switches and supply ramp need a plug-in current trace. Do not connect the module's own USB port while the wired external port is connected.

The later U9 BQ24392 addition must also be counted: it remains powered to retain USB data connectivity, and its required10k CHG_AL_N pullup to3.3V draws about0.33mA when asserted on an SDP/CDP. GOOD_BAT stays on always-on3.3V. The earlier module-only audit does not absorb this added load into a proven budget; the same complete-device2.5mA measurement remains required. See [BC detector review](bc12-detector-review.md).

## Clock and wake path

Simply changing the CPU to 12 MHz and executing WFI leaves unused clock trees enabled. RP2040 only enters system SLEEP when both cores are asleep and DMA is idle; SLEEP_EN gates then replace WAKE_EN. On an interrupt the hardware restores the WAKE_EN clocks before allowing the core back onto the bus. Keep WAKE_EN unchanged. B2 launches no second-core workload and uses no DMA. [RP2040 datasheet sections2.11.2 and2.15](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2040/rp2040-datasheet.pdf).

The installed Pico SDK's `pico_low_power/low_power.c` explicitly retains both `CLK_DEST_SYS_USBCTRL` and `CLK_DEST_USB_USBCTRL` for TinyUSB, and its sleep helper sets processor SLEEPDEEP around WFI. The B2 implementation now follows that pattern while retaining TIMER and WATCHDOG clock destinations for the core's timekeeping. It saves and restores SLEEP_EN0, SLEEP_EN1 and SCR, uses SLEEP_EN0=0 and SLEEP_EN1=SYS_USBCTRL|USB_USBCTRL|SYS_TIMER|SYS_WATCHDOG, and leaves the 48 MHz USB clock/PLL running. The system PLL is disabled after moving clk_sys to the 12 MHz reference. [Official SDK implementation](https://github.com/raspberrypi/pico-sdk/blob/master/src/rp2_common/pico_low_power/low_power.c).

The wait rechecks the suspend flag under `save_and_disable_interrupts()`, executes DSB/WFI, restores masks/SCR, then restores interrupt state. A pending enabled interrupt can wake WFI while PRIMASK suppresses handler entry; this avoids restoring clocks from a handler after it has already begun. No concrete source-level fault was found in this sequence. A pending periodic interrupt may shorten sleep and increase current; runtime behaviour matters. The firmware does not use dormant mode or the SDK clock-switch helper that deinitializes USB. USB reset/resume must retain the USB controller, its PLL/clock and interrupt path.

## Acceptance measurements still required

Measure **the complete display at VBUS**, with the stock modules wired exactly as specified, no battery, all LED-driver rails off and firmware's Plus indicator controls low. Check suspend both before configuration and after normal configuration, long enough to include any charge-indicator cycle and periodic wakeups. The displayed current must settle below the project's 2.5 mA suspend target with margin and enter the required low-power state promptly. A USB power meter that misses short pulses is insufficient for entry/inrush timing; use a current trace or suitable instrument.

Then repeatedly suspend/resume/reset the host and verify enumeration, CDC reflash, artwork restart and driver-rail sequencing. Repeat with both USB-C orientations and supported A-to-C/C-to-C supplies. Confirm no unexpected light remains on the module. If the measured budget fails, do not call B2 functionally equivalent merely because it compiles: investigate periodic IRQs, flash/charger draw and USB PLL current before committing to that module. No claim here relies on a generic XIAO deep-sleep figure measured with USB disconnected.
