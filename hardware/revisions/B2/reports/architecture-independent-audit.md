# Independent B2 architecture audit against A1

29 October 2026. Read electrical-review, the actual electrical graph/firmware, native module CAD and mechanical contract. This is an engineering review, not an order release or a physical test.

The material compatibility regression found was **A-to-C charger qualification**: the RP2040 has no native BC1.2 detector, so the earlier B2 would run a valid charging adapter dim while A1 could positively identify it and run full brightness. Root approved restoring that function with BQ24392. The source/SCH now includes U9 and separate upstream/downstream USB nets; its corrected firmware policy accepts only DET HIGH and AL_N LOW. The initially proposed AL_N HIGH fallback was rejected before compilation. See [detector review](bc12-detector-review.md). Final routed integration and real charger/USB tests are still required.

No inherent incompatibility was found in **3.3V TLC logic with a5V LED supply**. TI specifies3–5.5V VCC and independent sink outputs supporting17V. At3.3V, the7.42mA setting is within the2–35mA recommended range. RP2040's guaranteed2.62V output HIGH exceeds the TLC's2.31V input requirement. The PMOS gates are pulled up to their switched5V source and pulled down by separate TLC outputs, rather than exposing MCU pins to5V. A sink loaded by roughly1mA of gate-pullup current does not force the programmed7.42mA through a nonexistent load. This gate-driver use is an inference from the output specification;30µs settling/ghosting still needs the already-required waveform test. [TI TLC59282 specification](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlc59282.pdf), [AO3401A gate/current ratings](https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/datasheets/AO3401A.pdf).

The final routing uses an explicit **CT_5V-only exception on In2**, after the four signal-layer escape regions proved closed. The quiet timing-capacitor bridge is about 9.754 mm long, with 19 segments, normal 0.11 mm trace/0.15 mm clearance and no additional vias. Refilled In2 GND remains one connected outline; In4 remains the uninterrupted ground reference for USB on In3. The read-only audit caps this one exception at 12 mm and rejects every other non-GND track on In2/In4. This is a routing tradeoff, not a pin/function change or a clearance waiver.

The owner separately confirmed both full diode-test sequences on one `LMD07057AUE-101B-02`: all 12 pin roles/order match the design, with the part-number label on the left in the upright front view. The other 20 modules and powered-board behavior remain unverified. [Recorded owner observations](owner-led-diode-test-2026-10-29.json).

All245 binary pixels, arbitrary patterns, original39 commands, scroll/flash timing and60fps artwork scheduling are preserved by the independent pin-graph and player checks. After the approved6.8k IREF substitution, B2's calculated full-white LED average is342.0mA versus A1's341.6mA, approximately0.1% higher nominally. There is no known large imposed brightness cap, but exact apparent brightness has not been established for either unbuilt design. The unknown-source fallback is intentionally lower:18.2mA LED average versus A1's42.7mA. That remains a visible difference on an unqualified supply and should be disclosed; valid BC1.2 chargers, configured computers and qualifying Type-C supplies have the full-brightness path. Do not claim measured optical equivalence or raise the fallback until the total100mA budget is verified.

The front window, seven touching modules, hidden88.4×17.3mm mounting PCB and93.6×22.6×24mm case envelope remain. The bottom-right USB port moves4.65mm left. Native MCU corner contacts, socket overhang, screws, main-board stops and wire envelopes are checked. The stock-module mounting has useful rear-cover access, but pre-solder the XIAO backside USB pads **before** placing it in its cradle; those pads are then underneath it. Mount BOOT/RESET facing the removable cover and initially program the loose module. The external USB path supports later CDC/boot-ROM recovery; the module's own socket must stay unused when the external socket is wired.

The later mechanical review found a concrete retention gap: the rear cover had a clearance lip but no latch. Four rounded bead-and-pocket detents now provide a positive geometric closure within the same outer dimensions. The nominal seated CAD is collision-free, while closing deliberately requires approximately 0.20 mm shell deflection. All five print meshes, electronics clearances and MCU insertion sweep pass their nominal checks. The separate closure coupon and empty full case must still be printed to check force, retention under USB cable loads and repeated opening; these properties have not been measured. See the [mechanical assembly guide](../mechanical/README.md).

The assembly tradeoff is real: **20 wires and40 wire-end joints**, plus84 LED joints, make **124 owner solder joints per display** (372 for three) if fully self-assembled. The two new BC status wires account for four of those joints. These are14 MCU wires plus six USB-breakout wires; there are no owner-fitted QFN or other small components. The1.143mm XIAO USB pads and1.8×1.1mm rear main-board pads need controlled soldering and wire strain relief. They are less convenient than A1's factory-fitted connectors even though the programming procedure is simpler.

A **prewired module loom** can preserve the same case/front and remove the40 wire-end joints from the owner's work if a local assembler supplies the XIAO, breakout and main board already wired and continuity checked. It is a manual assembly service, not an item included in a55-SMT JLC quote or a verified off-the-shelf compatible harness. A labelled/pre-tinned wire kit makes handling easier but does not reduce joint count. Ordinary Dupont/header looms do not fit the underside height allowance and cannot plug directly into the present surface pads. Adding plug connectors requires another pad/height/routing review and should not be represented as a drop-in option.

No additional concrete topology fault was found in the reviewed startup/shutdown rules: serial controls stay low while TLC power is absent; BLANK pullup follows switched3V3; both TLC and LED rails shut down; GOOD_BAT uses always3V3; host suspend overrides all source grants. Complete-device current, added detector/pullup suspend load, MOSFET/anode settling, enclosed temperatures, USB data through the assembled loom and the owned LED variant remain prototype acceptance measurements already identified by the design. The CAD/emulator cannot certify them.
