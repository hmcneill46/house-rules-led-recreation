HOUSE RULES - FINE SPACING TRIALS D / E / F

Use LED_Pitch_DEF_Fine_KE04_HyperPLA.gcode.
ONE file prints all three full seven-module strips and a 50.00 mm scale coupon.

Printer: Creality Ender-3 V3 KE, stock 0.4 mm nozzle.
Material: Creality Hyper PLA grey, 1.75 mm. Do not use this file for PETG.
Settings: 0.20 mm layers, 100% scale, no supports or brim.
Nozzle: 220 C first layer, 215 C thereafter.
Bed: 60 C first layer, 55 C thereafter.
Slicer estimate: 1 hour 24 minutes 20 seconds; 11.24 g of filament.

Raised labels on each strip:
  D / P12.825 / +0.125   centre pitch 12.825 mm
  E / P12.850 / +0.150   centre pitch 12.850 mm
  F / P12.875 / +0.175   centre pitch 12.875 mm
The + value is the increase per join from the old 12.700 mm pitch.
Each module's original 12-hole pattern is unchanged, with 1.0 mm print holes.

Copy the G-code onto your printer's USB drive and start it manually.
Let the prints cool before removal. Check that each empty strip lies flat.
Measure the 50 mm coupon with calipers and keep the reading.
Try the same seven LEDs in the same order and orientation in each strip.
Keep modules square and insertion depth consistent; do not force the pins.
Choose the smallest pitch that seats all seven without bowing or pressure.
Report the winning letter D/E/F and the coupon measurement.
If the differences are inconclusive or empty strips bow, report that instead.

The 0.025 mm steps are nominal CAD values, not a promise of printer accuracy.
Adjacent trials differ by 0.150 mm across all six joins.
The label tab is outside the LED area. These are fitting tools, not case parts.
The current PCB/case remains on hold until the chosen pitch is applied/rechecked.
