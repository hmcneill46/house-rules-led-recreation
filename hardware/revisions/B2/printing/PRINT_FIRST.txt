House Rules B2 — Ender-3 V3 KE / 0.4 mm nozzle / Hyper PLA grey 1.75 mm

Unzip this pack, then copy the three .gcode files to the printer's USB drive.
Start each print at the printer. These files are for PLA.

Print 01 first: MCU channel, two-piece closure test, 1.6 mm dummy PCB and
50 mm scale coupon. Estimate: 38m 22s / 4.80 g. Check the actual stock modules
and measured dimensions before continuing.

Print 02: shell, 52m 39s / 12.24 g. Gently remove the outside brim and the
internal support strips. Keep the permanent PCB ledges intact.

Print 03: back and retaining cap, 30m 49s / 4.77 g. No supports are placed
inside the narrow MCU channels.

Nozzle: 220 C first layer / 215 C thereafter. Bed: 60 C / 55 C.
Model layers: 0.20 mm. Scale: 100%. Native KE homing, bed mesh, purge and
parking are retained. Real heating and levelling add time to the estimates.
No printer was connected to or started remotely.

Stock boards: one of each per display, without fitted headers.
Seeed XIAO RP2040 Plus (SKU 100059977):
https://www.unmannedtechshop.co.uk/products/seeed-studio-xiao-rp2040-plus
Adafruit 4090 USB-C downstream breakout:
https://thepihut.com/products/adafruit-usb-c-breakout-board-downstream-connection-ada4090
Leave any loose pin headers unfitted.

Full support-removal, fit-test and assembly instructions:
https://github.com/hmcneill46/house-rules-led-recreation/tree/main/hardware/revisions/B2/printing

Dummy PCB: 88.4 x 17.3 x 1.6 mm, with 84 pin centres matching the PCB.
Its 1.0 mm FDM holes differ from the real PCB's 0.90 mm finished holes.
It does not simulate copper, components or solder. Test everything unpowered;
do not force the cover against leads or misplaced boards.
