HOUSE RULES B2 CONFIRMED F - ALL PARTS ON ONE PLATE

Use B2_F_AllParts_OnePlate_KE04_HyperPLA.gcode.
ONE file prints the shell, rear cover, MCU cap and all four fit-test objects.
The closure coupon contains two pieces; they are one positioned mesh object.

Printer: Ender-3 V3 KE, stock 0.4 mm nozzle.
Material: Creality Hyper PLA grey 1.75 mm. This file is not for PETG.
Estimate: 1 hour 58 minutes 37 seconds, 21.94 g of filament.
Layers: 0.20 mm, 100% scale, by layer (not sequential whole objects).
Nozzle: 220 C first layer, then 215 C. Bed: 60 C first, then 55 C.
Only the shell has supports and a 3 mm outside brim.
All other objects have supports and brim disabled individually.

Copy the G-code to the printer USB drive and start it manually.
Let the plate cool before removing the parts. Remove shell supports/brim.
Measure the 50 mm coupon and the 89.45 x 17.30 x 1.60 mm PCB dummy.
Fit holes are 1.0 mm FDM holes; real PCB holes will be 0.90 mm finished.
Dry-fit the actual stock MCU/USB boards, screws and USB cable without power.
Check the cover closes/reopens and all parts seat without force.
Follow the mechanical guide for wire allowances, lead trimming and assembly.
No printer has been connected or started remotely, and no PCB order was placed.
