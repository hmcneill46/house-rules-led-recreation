This revision keeps all seven owned LED matrices and the88.4 ×17.3mm custom carrier and replaces the integrated controller/USB carrier with stock modules. ## Driver topology and compatibility

Four TI TLC59282 drivers sink the 49 independent row cathodes in local banks of two, two, two and one matrices. The five matching column anodes are shared across the seven matrices and each is driven by one AO3401A P-channel MOSFET. This reproduces the five-column multiplex arrangement with adjustable PWM brightness and arbitrary 7 × 35 artwork. There is no MAX7219 polarity adaptation or fixed-font controller limitation.

The raw module pin assignment remains: visual left-to-right column anodes `[1,3,10,7,8]`; visual top-to-bottom cathodes `[12,11,2,9,4,5,6]`. The owner confirmed both complete diode-test sequences on one owned `LMD07057AUE-101B-02` on 29 October 2026: all12 terminal roles/order match, with the printed part-number label on the **left** in the correct upright front view. This is owner-reported physical evidence, not an emulator result. The other20 modules, powered-board operation and actual assembly fit still need checking. See the [recorded observations](reports/owner-led-diode-test-2026-10-29.json).

U1/U2/U3 each use OUT0..13 for two modules' seven rows; OUT14..15 remain disconnected. U4 uses OUT0..6 for module7 and OUT7..11 to pull the five MOSFET gates low. Gate pullups are 4.7k to the **switched** LED rail. This removes five separate NPN level shifters, their resistors and five MCU column wires. Each selected gate consumes about1.06 mA at5 V. Each column also has a10k anode-to-ground bleed, consuming0.5 mA only for the selected column, to discharge inactive anode capacitance.

Using a TLC output to pull a gate low is an engineering inference from the specified constant-current sink behavior, not a separately advertised TI gate-driver application. At a1 V gate voltage, the programmed sink can pull about7.42 mA while its4.7k pullup supplies only0.85 mA. The gate therefore falls below1 V, giving more than3.5 V of negative gate drive at a4.5 V LED supply. AO3401A on-resistance is specified even at−2.5 V gate drive. Its±12 V gate rating easily contains the5 V supply. At364 mA peak,85mΩ gives about31 mV drop and11 mW instantaneous conduction loss.

The pullup/AO3401A typical645pF input-capacitance time constant is about3.03µs;30µs is almost10 time constants in a simple small-signal approximation. MOSFET Miller charge and actual wiring make that an estimate, not a guaranteed off-time. Gate and anode waveforms must be scoped on the prototype. The10k bleeds and30µs blank are intended to suppress ghosting; increase blank time only if measured settling requires it, then compare actual brightness to A1.

## Serial/firmware contract

The chain is MCU→U1→U2→U3→U4. Transmit U4's word first and U1's word last, OUT15 first within each16-bit word. Global row bits are0..13,16..29,32..45,48..54. Column-gate bits are55..59. Unused bits14,15,30,31,46,47,60..63 are always zero. An active phase contains exactly one gate bit. A set row bit enables its cathode sink; a set gate bit enables its anode MOSFET through the gate sink.

BLANK HIGH forces every output off. LAT is level-sensitive: keep it LOW while shifting, pulse HIGH after64 bits, then return LOW before unblanking. Latch new data while blanked and leave at least30µs total blanking between lit columns. Power-on latch contents are unspecified, so clear/latch a full64 zero bits before any light output.

## Brightness, current and temperature

IREF=6.8k1% gives a nominal peak LED current `1.205×41.9/6.8 = 7.42493mA`. Basic C23212 replaces the earlier Extended6.98k part, avoiding its separate setup fee while bringing nominal full-white brightness close to A1. Each500µs phase comprises30µs blank plus up to470µs light. Five phases give a400 Hz frame. A pixel's maximum average duty is18.8%, close to the A1 five-column multiplex operating point. There is no70% brightness cap in B2.

Full-white LED current is about363.82 mA instantaneous and341.99 mA average at94% within-phase light duty. Add approximately1.47 mA average for the selected gate pullup and anode bleed, about0.66 mA for BLANK's pullup when driven LOW, driver logic, MCU and module overhead. Using the TLC datasheet's conservative15mA on/6mA blank supply-current test bounds (measured at a higher16.8mA LED setting) gives57.84 mA for four drivers at94% light duty. An estimated30mA MCU/module allowance yields roughly432mA before small remaining loads. This is a planning estimate, not a certified500mA maximum: resistor/reference spread, source voltage, MCU workload, regulator losses and actual temperature must be measured.

At an illustrative5 V supply and2 V red LED drop, a14-channel driver dissipates about0.312 W while lit; at94% duty plus the conservative logic allowance, about0.34 W. The selected six-layer candidate connects EP25 to GND22 and uses three filled/capped0.30mm thermal barrels per driver,12 total, with In4 GND-only, In2 primarily GND around the short CT_5V timing-capacitor bridge, and In3 signal routing plus GND fill. These improve the heat path, but TI's QFN46.8°C/W standardized-board thermal figure is still **not a measured thermal resistance for this narrow carrier inside its case**. No junction-temperature guarantee follows from multiplying that figure by0.34 W. At5.5 V and an unrealistically shorted LED load,14×7.425mA×5.5 V≈0.572 W before logic: that is a useful conservative fault-power bound, not normal optical operation. Verify full-white operation in the printed case at the intended ambient temperature, including the stock module's LDO; check both driver temperature and nearby plastic. Default dim playback produces far less heat.

At5% within-phase light duty, LED average current is18.19mA and the same conservative driver-current model is25.8mA; with a30mA module allowance the subtotal is about74.0mA. The accepted prototype firmware defaults to25µs DIM when source authorization is unknown, with a compile-time unknown-source OFF alternative. This is an unqualified current-budget choice pending measurement; it is not a claim of demonstrated<100mA operation. A stuck single phase does not exceed the matrix's20mA continuous LED rating at the chosen7.42mA peak, but watchdog and USB power-state handling are still required.

## USB-C, inrush and suspend

Use5 V USB-C power with USB2 data and **no USB Power Delivery voltage negotiation**. Adafruit4090 already has one5.1k Rd on each CC; do not fit duplicate Rd resistors. Ordinary C-to-C and A-to-C cables can be supported. The XIAO's own USB socket remains unused when the external socket is wired; connecting both risks two hosts/sources being joined.

CC1 and CC2 each use a47k/47k divider and10nF filter to GPIO26/27. The divider loads Rd with94k: nominal equivalent Rd≈4.84k. Even a5%-low5.1k Rd with1%-low divider resistance is approximately4.61k, just above the4.59k lower limit for a5.1k±10% sink resistor. This loading must be included rather than treating the ADC as an ideal open circuit. The divider source resistance23.5k and10nF filter give235µs nominal settling; sample after settling and calibrate against the actual3.3 V ADC reference. Do not infer a BC1.2 charging-port grant from VBUS alone; the RP2040 module provides no built-in BC1.2 detector; the added U9 supplies detection.

USB data and both CC signals retain the A1 TPD4E05U06 low-capacitance ESD shunt, now on the main carrier. The opposite NC pads are joined to matching signal nets by external copper; they are not internally connected flow-through paths. The USB data pair must run from the raw breakout pads past the ESD shunt to the MCU pads, with short ground return. A SMF5.0A shunts VBUS transients. The750mA PPTC is fault protection, not a grant to draw750mA and not DC overvoltage protection.

TLC59282 has no true shutdown input: four blanked devices can still consume up to24mA under its datasheet test condition. Two TPS22918DBVR switches therefore disable both driver3.3 V and the LED5 V rail. This also avoids depending on unspecified TLC OUT leakage at VCC=0. Each switch has QOD tied directly to VOUT,1nF CT, common GPIO6 enable and an external100k EN pulldown. CT=1nF gives a typical5 V rise of2.54ms and3.3 V rise of1.68ms; firmware waits10ms before initialization, subject to measured worst-case ramp. Switch shutdown-current maxima are only a fewµA each; the **whole XIAO module** still needs a measured USB suspend budget.

Shutdown: BLANK HIGH → wait≥30µs → SIN/SCLK/LAT LOW → release BLANK to input high impedance with no internal pulls → DRIVER_EN LOW. Wake: controls LOW, BLANK high impedance → DRIVER_EN HIGH → wait≥10ms → BLANK HIGH → shift/latch zeros → authorized scan. Never drive a logic HIGH into an unpowered TLC. BLANK's4.7k pullup is to switched3.3 V only. The switch EN defaults LOW during MCU reset; rails stay off until firmware explicitly enables them.

Raw main5 V input capacitance is1µF; switched LED capacitance is4.7µF. Driver-switch input/output bulk is1µF each plus four100nF TLC bypasses. The stock XIAO has1.1µF directly on VBUS and14.8µF on VSYS after its TPS2116 mux, plus downstream LDO capacitance. To include that module load in controlled startup, **a third TPS22918 (U8)** is fitted before the entire+5V rail, including the XIAO feed. Its VIN and ON use fused raw VBUS, CT=1nF, QOD=VOUT; the1µF C14 is its raw input bypass. It reuses the same factory part number as the other rail switches. A roughly30µF total load charged through a typical2.54ms5 V ramp would draw about59mA capacitive charging current; actual mux/LDO start thresholds and MCU load are nonlinear. **Direct-capacitance addition alone cannot prove USB inrush compliance**; measure the real input-current/charge waveform. Both USB500mA configured operation and host suspend must be verified on the actual hardware; darkness alone does not establish compliant low-power suspend.

## Restored A-to-C charger qualification

U9 BQ24392RSER restores the native A1 BC1.2 CDP/DCP qualification lost by using an RP2040 module alone. It is factory-fitted in the TI RSE0010A ten-land1.5×2.0×0.6mm package (no separate EP11). It receives always-on+5V with C15=100n and C16=1u local bypass; GOOD_BAT is hard-tied to always-on+3V3, never SW_3V3. That keeps its data path connected on an SDP/CDP while the LED rails are off for host suspend. Its pin1 SW_OPEN output is intentionally unused.

Raw connector D+ and D− go through the ESD region to U9 pins7/8; downstream pins3/2 go separately to XIAO TP4/TP12. The switch connects automatically for an SDP/CDP after detection. A DCP stays isolated from the MCU USB pins. USB power/programming still use the same external connector. Read both status pins: **BC_DET HIGH and BC_AL_N LOW**, stable100ms, positively grants the project's500mA operating target; any other combination withdraws that grant. In particular AL_N HIGH means charging is not allowed, even if DET is HIGH. A real host suspend overrides BC, USB configuration and CC grants. The detector handles data-contact and primary/secondary detection; very slow insertion can conservatively leave an otherwise valid charger unqualified.

CHG_DET pin10 is3.5V…VBUS push-pull and cannot connect directly to the RP2040. R21=10k upper/R22=15k lower give a0.6 divider. With1% resistors and1µA input leakage, the independent model bounds HIGH to2.077…3.181V for the specified3.5…5.25V detector range, above the RP2040's **2.0V** VIH and below the3.3V regulator's conservative minimum. R23=10k pulls the open-drain CHG_AL_N up to always3V3. On an SDP/CDP that pullup alone adds about0.33mA and the powered detector also consumes current, so the complete suspend measurement must include both. The static model checks the terminal map, USB separation, GOOD_BAT supply and divider limits; it does not simulate USB transactions.

The authoritative source tables and firmware policy review are in [bc12-detector-review.md](reports/bc12-detector-review.md). JLC C128408 was independently confirmed in live assembly search at937 units and$0.4226 qty1 on29 October; availability is not reserved.

## Wire contract and physical integration

MCU J1..12, plus J19/J20 BC status wires: `+5V, GND, +3V3, SIN, SCLK, LAT, BLANK, DRIVER_EN, CC1_ADC, CC2_ADC, USB_DM_HOST, USB_DP_HOST` respectively, with J19=BC_DET and J20=BC_AL_N. The XIAO basic power pads are14/13/12 for5V/GND/3V3. USB D− is TP12 and D+ is TP4. Breakout J13..18: `VBUS, GND, CC1, CC2, USB_DM, USB_DP`, connected to Adafruit4090 pads2/1/4/8/3/5 respectively. Numbering is by pad label, not an assumed visual cable order. U7 remains an ESD shunt on raw USB_DM/USB_DP. U9 is a genuine series USB isolation switch; its downstream USB_DM_HOST/USB_DP_HOST nets are separate and reach J11/J12. Never join these raw and downstream pairs with bypass copper. J19/J20 carry BC_DET/BC_AL_N to XIAO pads6/7 (GP7/GP0).

Wire pads are**rear1.8 ×1.1mm rounded SMD lands, without drills or paste openings**, owner soldered with28AWG flexible insulated wire. They are excluded from factory placement despite the SMD footprint attribute. The84 LED through-holes remain unchanged. This keeps wire stubs off the visible front and avoids collisions with the LED leads and front-side courtyards. Pre-tin each wire and pad, keep the exposed conductor short, and support the wire while soldering. Add strain relief using case adhesive tacks/keepers so cable pulls do not peel these lands. Keep USB data wires together and away from scan-current loops. The stock breakout and XIAO fit analysis is in the companion module report: the port remains bottom-right but its candidate centre moves about4.65mm left to keep the stock USB PCB within the display silhouette.

## Package selection and verification

DBQR SSOP24 was investigated because its leads are inspectable. A native KiCad search of10,626 positions/orientations found no clearance-valid placement; the best still overlapped LED copper by0.079mm. The selected **TLC59282RGER** QFN24 at0.5mm pitch has a different terminal map and an exposed ground pad. The JSON records both exact maps and selects RGER consistently. TI's updated RGE0024C drawing uses a**2.1mm square exposed pad**, not the2.7mm pad of another similarly named RGE option. Root owns the custom `TI:RGE0024C` land pattern. Its exposed pad25 must connect to ground; it is not internally connected to the GND pin. The selected six-layer candidate uses0.30mm drill/0.60mm pad vias, with0.45mm pads at selected escapes. All0.30mm vias are filled and capped;12 of them are GND thermal barrels in the four exposed pads. All84 LED through-holes are0.90mm and must remain open. These are separate hole classes: never request blanket filling of every drilled hole. The selected plane intent is In4 GND-only, In2 primarily GND with a single approved **CT_5V-only** timing-capacitor bridge, and In3 signals including USB plus GND fill. The reference bridge uses19 segments, **9.753863mm** total at0.11mm width, normal0.15mm copper clearance and no additional vias. The four ordinary signal layers were enclosed at this timing node; the quiet bridge is an explicit routing exception, not a relaxed clearance or USB/clock route on the reference plane. Its refilled In2 GND remains one connected filled outline; In4 provides the unchanged ground reference for USB on In3. The read-only audit limits total In2 CT length to12mm and rejects every other non-GND net on In2/In4. Final routed files need a fresh manufacturing quote.

`python3 hardware/revisions/B2/tools/build_schematics.py --verify` generated the editable QFN/three-switch B2 schematic and passed **zero ERC violations without exclusions**, plus exact exported-netlist parity for all**327 connected pins** across82 components.
