Scope: digital diode connectivity, proposed IS31FL3729 register allocation,
shutdown logic, and datasheet-based current calculations. This is not an
analogue or MCU-machine-code emulator and is not a hardware test certificate.

Coverage:
- All 245 walking pixels reconstructed through independently defined module diodes.
- Checkerboard and all-on patterns; all five scan phases partition the 245 dots.
- Wrong source/sink polarity rejected; swapped rows produce a visible mismatch.
- Shared pair cathodes rejected because physical pixels alias a register address.
- Driver package pins and generated pixel CSV checked against the derived topology.
- Used/unused outputs, startup/reset, software and hardware shutdown.
- I2C 10 us quiet guards before and after SDB rising.
- 47 kohm hardware peak setting, including 1% low resistor tolerance.
- USB pre-authorisation LEDs off; configured 500 mA permits the nominal full frame.
- Lower authorised average-current budget caps PWM; authorisation loss blanks LEDs.
- Firmware default-current GCC8 fallback stays below 100 mA with nominal 30 mA reserve.
- Only completed positive BC1.2 CDP/DCP results grant charger current; host suspend blanks.
- Unmodelled all-switches-on mode raises instead of understating current.
