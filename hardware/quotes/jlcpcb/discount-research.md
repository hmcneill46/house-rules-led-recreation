## How to establish the cheapest genuine total

Use one frozen manufacturing specification for both websites/app and all quantity comparisons. Compare the final **delivered and taxed amount**, after selecting one actually eligible coupon. Check whether the main and carrier can share shipping without losing a larger discount. A second shipment to use another coupon only saves money if its full extra freight/tax is smaller than the saving. Do not assume two board jobs permit two coupons in one payment batch.

JLCPCB says coupons are service-specific and normally limited to **one coupon per order batch**. Eligibility appears after shipping information at checkout; multiple coupons cannot be added together. The documented JLCONE automatic instant discount is the relevant exception to ordinary coupon stacking. [Coupon-use rules](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/how-do-i-use-a-coupon-for-my-order).

## Why the main board can exceed the advertised six-layer headline price

The frozen main board uses 0.20 mm drill / 0.40 mm copper-diameter vias. JLCPCB's live quote help identifies that exact combination as chargeable small vias. The free multilayer 0.20 mm option needs at least 0.45 mm copper diameter. Free VIPPO does not imply the small-via surcharge disappears. A 0.30 mm drill inside the existing 0.40 mm copper would reduce the annular ring and is a design change, not a valid quoting shortcut. [Official quote specification help](https://cart.jlcpcb.com/quote/?fromDemo=yes).

Use the exact fabrication/assembly result from the quoting task to determine the actual 1-each and 3-each minimum payable totals. The public promotions alone cannot provide those totals.
